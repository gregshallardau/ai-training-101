<deck-ideas-map>
key views & moments

A force-directed graph of ideas that recurs through the deck, from a dim star map (slide 3) to a lit constellation on the context slides. Clusters are colour-coded by topic and overlap; nodes drag; a custom force keeps the word2vec regularity — one step in a fixed direction is the same relationship everywhere, across topics (man→woman in family is the same step as bull→cow in animals). Each frame is one attribute-driven state of the same graph.
