One recurring map threaded through the deck, visualising Mikolov et al. 2013 (word2vec): distance is similarity, and a relationship is a constant offset vector. Words sit at authored coordinates; regions are the enclosing circles of their words, and the camera is the zoomable-circle-packing fly-in. Each frame is one attribute-driven state of the same map.

Static mock — no force simulation, coordinates hand-placed so the analogy parallelograms read and each cluster's enclosing circle barely overlaps its siblings. In the real component every word carries authored x/y; each region circle is d3.packEnclose() over its children; the camera lifts zoomTo / d3.interpolateZoom verbatim from reference/d3-circle-pack.md. Overlays redraw without recomputing geometry, so the map looks identical every time it appears.
