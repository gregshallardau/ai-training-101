The Meaning of Words - Word Association

This is not about memorising every label. Start with a page of nearby dots and one known relationship: girl to boy gives the female-to-male direction. Ask: what is a female horse called? Reveal stallion by activating one of the grey dots as the known male point. Then use the same arrow backwards from stallion until it hits another existing grey dot. Ask: what is that dot? Let them say mare before revealing and activating the label. The last press makes the neighbourhood readable: mare/stallion, filly/colt, foal, horse. The point is that meaning is geometry: if you know the direction, you can search the space for the missing word.
