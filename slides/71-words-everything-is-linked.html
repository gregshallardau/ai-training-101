Everything Is Linked

"Why not just train it on our topic — don't give it science when we're doing arts?"

Sounds reasonable. It's wrong.

Researchers have stripped other fields out of a model's training data — and the responses on the target field got worse, not better.

Everything appears to be linked.

Citation, verified: Longpre et al., "A Pretrainer's Guide to Training Data: Measuring the Effects of Data Age, Domain Coverage, Quality, & Toxicity" (NAACL 2024, arXiv:2305.13169). They pretrained 28 1.5B-param models varying domain composition and found domain heterogeneity often beats targeted data even on targeted evaluations — removing a domain degrades performance on evaluations outside it, matching the "removed a field, responses got worse" claim on this slide.
