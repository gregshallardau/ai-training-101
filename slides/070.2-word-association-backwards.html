Now flip one arrow. The faint forward arrow (Italy → Rome) is already there; press once and the same line appears pointing the other way — Rome → Italy — with the "? ← Rome" cue. If "Rome" is the word you're given, the answer is one vector-step behind it: Italy. The arrow doesn't care which end you start from — same line, same length, direction reversed. Press again: x + 5 = 7. You don't reach for a library of "5 → 7" facts — you solve: x = 7 − 5. Same with words: given the answer side, walk the arrow backwards to the word that leads to it. Let the room solve it out loud. Final press, the payoff: a vector is a direction and a distance.
