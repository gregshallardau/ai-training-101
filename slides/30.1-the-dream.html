The dream is old. In 1950 Alan Turing asks whether machines can think, in 1956 the phrase “artificial intelligence” is coined, and by 1966 there are primitive chatbots. Almost none of it works yet — but the question is set.

Stop 1 of 6. Turing's "imitation game" (1950); Dartmouth workshop coins "artificial intelligence" (1956); ELIZA (1966) — a chatbot that mostly echoed your sentences back. Point: talking to machines is a 70-year-old idea, not a 2022 one. Don't linger — it all goes quiet for decades after this.
