The Glossary

Token
a chunk of text. A word.
Vector
a direction and a distance. Similar words (or ideas) point the same way.
Weight
a dial. How strongly two tokens are connected.
Model
all the dial positions together. The complete knowledge.
Prompt
what you type. Where you start.
Context
everything that came before. The situation around the words.
Large Language Model
a model trained on a huge amount of text and conversation.
Multi-Modal
reads more than words — pictures, voice, files too.
System Prompt
the instructions the tool gives itself before you type.
Prompt Engineering
the old art of wording the perfect question.
Context Engineering
the new skill: bringing who you are and what you need to the question.
Hallucination
when it makes things up — sure of itself.
