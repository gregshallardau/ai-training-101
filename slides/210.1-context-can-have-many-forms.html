Context Can Have Many Forms

The context you give an AI doesn't have to be typed.

It takes context in many forms — typed words, Word docs, PDFs, Excel sheets, PowerPoint, images, video, audio — and turns all of them into the same tokens.

The context matrix

The same eight questions from "Context in the Real World" — now each answer is tagged with what it actually is and the form it arrives in.

WHO · Sarah's client deck.pptx WHEN · claims_history.xlsx WHY · market commentary.pdf HOW · tone notes (typed) WHEN · scanned policy schedule (image)

Chip fill colour = the input type it arrived in. Chip border texture = which kind of context it answers (WHO / WHAT / WHEN / WHERE / WHY / HOW). The label always names the real artefact, so the colour is never floating without a reason.

  • Text-bearing documents (PDFs, Word docs, Excel, slides) — the model pulls the words out of them (OCR for a scan, an extraction step for a digital file), and from there they're just tokens, exactly like typed words.
  • Images, video and audio have no words to lift out — the model "sees" and "hears" them directly. Sketch: a projector beaming a picture onto the wall, scattering it into token chips on the far side.
  • Pay-off: paste the whiteboard photo, the client's PDF, or your spreadsheet straight in — that is context, just in another form.
  • Caution: garbage in, garbage out — verify what a file actually says. If it reads a claim PDF or a policy word-for-word, it will use words you didn't notice were in there.
Grounding for the wh-word borders (not shown on the slide): situation-model theory - Zwaan & Radvansky (1998), Psychological Bulletin; Hymes' SPEAKING grid. WHO = participants (solid border), WHAT = the action/state held (dashed), WHEN = timing/history (dotted), WHERE = place/setting (double), WHY = drivers/causation (groove), HOW = tone/register (ridge). Multi-kind facts: colour tags the medium, the border tags the fact. If one artefact feeds two layers (e.g. one Excel holding the client list AND the renewals), show two chips of the same colour, one per fact; a fact that genuinely straddles two kinds gets a compound label on one chip, e.g. "WHO + WHAT · client_tasks.xlsx" - one chip can't carry two border textures.
