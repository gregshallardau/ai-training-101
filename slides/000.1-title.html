AI Actual Intelligence

A plain-English look at the AI and how you use it in your day.

The spine of the whole session is a single predictor answering one specialty question. We meet it early giving a confident, slightly-wrong general-knowledge answer, then we add one layer of context at a time and watch that same answer climb. At the end we see the whole climb in one view. Keep pointing at the question, it never changes; only the context does.

Runs offline once loaded, press S for speaker view, click the coloured widgets live. The title slide runs an ambient looping animation (particles + a token travelling a small network) meant to play while people join — it pauses automatically once you move past this slide.
