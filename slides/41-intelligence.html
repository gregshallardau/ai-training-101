Intelligence

This is just intelligence, done by a computer. It thinks in a similar way to us.

In the past, computers were made with programming. AI is made to replicate our brains.

Neuron activation in a brain vs. the dials and activations in a neural network.

We trained our brains at school and through life — reading, listening to our teachers, getting feedback ("this draft is good" / "this one could be better") — and our brain changed over time.

This is how AI is trained: give it a whole bunch of information, then tell it "this is good" or "this could be better," until it develops to where it is now.
