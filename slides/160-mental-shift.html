What We've Built So Far

Token — a chunk of text.

Vector — where similar words live together.

Weight — how strongly two things are linked.

Context — everything that came before. The situation around the words.

Model — all the weights. Everything it knows.

Prompt — where you start. But it knows nothing on its own.

It's read more than any one person could in ten lifetimes — it knows everything about the world.

And nothing about you. — your name, your job, why you're asking, who it's for.

Same we saw in the picture game: same question, different context → totally different answer.

From the plan, if there's time for a live analogy here: you wouldn't walk up to your colleague and say "help me" and expect them to know what you need. You'd say "Hey, I've got this thing with Sarah next Friday. Can I get the day off? She's stressed about the campaign but I really need it." That's context — that's what you're doing when you talk to AI.
